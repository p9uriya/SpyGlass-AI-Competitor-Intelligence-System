# 🔍 SpyGlass AI
## Autonomous Competitor Intelligence System

An AI-powered competitor intelligence system built for Mammut to automate competitor monitoring, market research, AI analysis, and executive reporting.

---

## 🚀 Overview

SpyGlass AI is designed to monitor Mammut's competitors across Iranian and international markets.

The system automatically collects competitor and market signals, sends the collected data to an AI Agent for analysis, identifies potential threats and opportunities, and generates intelligence reports.

The final report is automatically delivered via email.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Monitoring competitors manually can be:

- ⏳ Time-consuming
- 📊 Difficult to organize
- 🔍 Limited by human research
- 🐌 Slow to detect important market changes

SpyGlass AI automates this process.
